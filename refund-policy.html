01

Introduction

At VORVENA, we aim to provide a fair and reliable environment for clients and technology professionals to work together.

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains when a client may request a cancellation or refund for a project paid through VORVENA and how such requests are reviewed.

By using VORVENA's hiring and payment services, you agree to follow this policy alongside our Terms of Service.

02

Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the stage of the project, the agreement between the client and professional, the work completed and the circumstances surrounding the request.

A refund may be considered where:

  • The project is cancelled before work begins.
  • The professional fails to begin or deliver the agreed work.
  • The professional materially fails to meet the agreed project requirements.
  • The professional cancels the project without completing the agreed work.
  • A payment was made incorrectly or duplicated.
  • Another circumstance justifies a refund under applicable law.

Submitting a refund request does not automatically guarantee approval. VORVENA will review the available information before making a decision.

03

Cancellation Before Work Begins

If a client requests cancellation before the professional has started the agreed work, the client may be eligible for a full refund.

VORVENA may verify whether work has started before approving the cancellation.

Any third-party payment processing fees will be handled according to the payment provider's rules and applicable law. Any deduction from a refund must be clearly explained and legally permitted.

04

Cancellation After Work Begins

If work has already started, a refund may be partial rather than full.

VORVENA may consider:

  • The amount of work already completed.
  • Project milestones and agreed deliverables.
  • Evidence of progress submitted by the professional.
  • The original project agreement.
  • Any communication between the client and professional.

The amount, if any, to be refunded will be determined after reviewing the circumstances of the cancellation.

05

Incomplete or Unsatisfactory Work

A client may request a review if the delivered work is incomplete or materially different from the agreed requirements.

Where appropriate, VORVENA may allow the professional an opportunity to correct the issue or complete the missing work.

Depending on the circumstances, the outcome may include:

  • Completion or correction of the work.
  • A partial refund.
  • A full refund where justified.
  • Another reasonable resolution permitted by applicable law.
06

Professional Cancellations

If a professional cancels an accepted project or becomes unable to complete the agreed work, the client may be eligible for a refund of the amount attributable to work not completed.

Where agreed milestones have already been completed and accepted, VORVENA may take those milestones into account when reviewing the refund.

07

Completed and Approved Work

Once a client has reviewed and approved the completed deliverables and the applicable payment has been released to the professional, refunds will generally not be available.

Exceptions may be considered in cases involving payment errors, fraud, serious misconduct or rights provided under applicable law.

08

How to Request a Refund

Clients who wish to request a refund should contact VORVENA through the official Contact Support page.

Please provide:

  • Your registered email address.
  • The project or transaction reference.
  • The reason for requesting a refund.
  • Relevant messages, documents or other supporting evidence.

VORVENA may request additional information where necessary to review the request fairly.

Contact VORVENA Support
09

Refund Processing

Approved refunds will generally be sent through the original payment method or another method supported by VORVENA and its payment provider.

VORVENA uses Paystack for supported payment processing. Actual refund processing times may depend on Paystack, the client's bank, card issuer or other financial institutions involved.

VORVENA cannot guarantee an exact refund arrival date where processing depends on third-party institutions.

10

Platform Commission and Professional Earnings

VORVENA's standard project commission is 15% of the applicable project payment, while the professional's share is 85%, subject to the agreed payment terms.

Where a refund is approved, VORVENA will adjust or reverse the relevant platform commission and professional earnings as necessary to reflect the approved refund.

Amounts that are subject to an unresolved refund review may be withheld from payout until the review is completed, where permitted by applicable law and payment provider rules.

11

Exceptions and Non-Refundable Situations

A refund may generally not be available where:

  • The client has approved the completed work and payment has been released.
  • The client changes their mind after the agreed work has been completed.
  • The request is based on requirements that were not part of the original agreement.
  • The client fails to provide information or materials necessary for the professional to complete the work.
  • The request involves misuse of the platform or fraudulent activity.

These situations are subject to review and do not limit any refund or consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

12

User Responsibilities

Clients and professionals are expected to communicate clearly, agree on project requirements before work begins and use VORVENA's official payment and project processes.

Users must provide accurate information and cooperate reasonably when a cancellation or refund request is being reviewed.

Users should not make false refund claims, submit misleading evidence or attempt to bypass VORVENA's payment process.

13

Changes to This Policy

VORVENA may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in its services, payment arrangements, operational requirements or applicable laws.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Where required, users will be notified of material changes.
