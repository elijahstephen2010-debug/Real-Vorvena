01

Introduction

Welcome to VORVENA. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of everyone who uses our platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, who we may share it with and the choices available to you.

By using VORVENA, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will request it separately.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use VORVENA, we may collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

  • Full name and email address.
  • Phone number and country.
  • Account type: Client or Professional.
  • Login credentials and account preferences.

Professional Profile Information

  • Professional title and work experience.
  • Skills, biography and qualifications.
  • Portfolio links and social media links.
  • Profile photograph and submitted documents.
  • SkillProof assessment results and verification status.

Project Information

  • Hiring offers and project descriptions.
  • Project deadlines, budgets and work submissions.
  • Messages and files exchanged through the workspace.
  • Project history, feedback and ratings.

Verification Information

Where required, we may collect identity verification information or documents to establish account authenticity and protect the platform from fraud.

03

How We Collect Information

We collect information through several methods:

  • When you register or update your account.
  • When you complete professional verification.
  • When you communicate with other users.
  • When you make or receive payments.
  • When you contact our support team.
  • Through cookies, security logs and similar technologies.

Some technical information, such as device type, browser information, IP address and platform activity, may also be collected to maintain security and improve our services.

04

How We Use Your Information

VORVENA may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Verify user identities and professional qualifications.
  • Connect clients with suitable professionals.
  • Process payments and manage project transactions.
  • Operate project workspaces and communication tools.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised access.
  • Respond to support requests and complaints.
  • Improve platform performance and user experience.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
05

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. However, some information may be shared when necessary to operate VORVENA.

With Other Users

Professional profile information intended for public display may be visible to clients. Information necessary to manage a project may be shared with the relevant participants.

We aim to limit unnecessary disclosure of client identifying information in professional hiring views.

With Service Providers

We may share relevant information with trusted providers that help us operate the platform, including payment processors, hosting providers, email services, identity verification providers and security services.

For Legal and Safety Reasons

Information may be disclosed where required by law, valid legal process or necessary to protect the rights, safety and security of VORVENA and its users.

06

Payments and Financial Information

VORVENA uses third-party payment providers, including Paystack, to facilitate supported transactions.

Payment information may be processed directly by the payment provider in accordance with its own privacy and security practices.

VORVENA does not intend to store users' complete card numbers, card security codes or other sensitive card authentication details on its own servers.

We may receive transaction references, payment status, amounts and other information necessary to manage payments, fees, refunds and professional earnings.

07

Identity and Professional Verification

VORVENA may require identity verification and professional assessments to establish trust and platform safety.

Verification may involve identity documents, submitted qualifications, assessment results and other relevant information.

Verification information will be accessed only by authorised personnel and relevant service providers where necessary. We will handle such information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

08

Data Protection and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration and disclosure.

These measures may include:

  • Access controls and account authentication.
  • Secure communication and data transmission.
  • Monitoring for suspicious activity.
  • Restricted access to sensitive information.
  • Security reviews and system updates.

No online system can guarantee absolute security. Users should also protect their login credentials and report suspicious account activity promptly.

09

Cookies and Similar Technologies

VORVENA may use cookies and similar technologies to support essential website functionality, remember preferences, maintain sessions and understand how users interact with the platform.

Where required, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies. You may also manage cookies through your browser settings.

10

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including account management, transactions, security, dispute handling and legal obligations.

Some records may need to be retained after account closure where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

11

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country and applicable data protection laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Request a copy of certain personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Submit a complaint to the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details provided below. Some requests may be limited by legal obligations or other lawful grounds.

12

International Data Transfers

VORVENA is designed for users in different countries. Your information may therefore be processed or stored in countries other than your country of residence.

Where international transfers occur, we will take appropriate steps to protect personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

13

Children's Privacy

VORVENA is intended for individuals who are at least 18 years old or the higher age of legal majority required in their country.

We do not knowingly permit individuals below the applicable minimum age to create accounts or use our professional marketplace services.

If we discover that an account has been created by someone who does not meet the applicable age requirement, we may restrict or close that account.

14

Third-Party Services and Links

VORVENA may contain links to external websites or integrate services operated by third parties.

These third parties maintain their own privacy policies and practices. We encourage users to review their policies before providing personal information to them.

VORVENA is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of external websites that we do not operate.

15

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as VORVENA develops, our services change or legal requirements evolve.

When we make material changes, we will take reasonable steps to notify affected users. Where required, we will request renewed consent before relying on that consent for the relevant processing.

The latest version will always be made available on this page, together with its effective date.

16

If you have questions, concerns or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact our support team.
